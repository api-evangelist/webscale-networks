# Webscale Networks
> Webscale is an AI-powered commerce infrastructure platform that helps ecommerce merchants run faster, more secure storefronts — with agentic AI tools for personalization, conversion, and autonomous operations.
Webscale delivers managed cloud hosting, application delivery, and a suite of agentic AI products built specifically for ecommerce. The platform supports Adobe Commerce, Magento, Shopware, and headless architectures. Customers include B2C, B2B, and regulated commerce businesses that require enterprise-grade performance, security, and AI-driven shopper experiences at scale.
